AI-Powered HR Assistant

Background
----------
Our organisation of ~4,000 employees receives thousands of repetitive HR
questions every month — about leave policy, payroll, benefits, and IT access.
The HR team spends a large share of its time answering the same questions and
manually raising support tickets. We want to introduce an AI-powered HR
Assistant to reduce this load and give employees instant, accurate answers.

Business Goal
-------------
Reduce HR ticket volume and response time by giving employees a self-service
AI assistant that answers policy questions and raises tickets automatically.

Target Users
------------
- Employees (primary)
- HR Team
- IT Helpdesk

Key Features
------------
1. Answer employee FAQs about HR policies in natural language.
2. Search and summarise company documents (policy PDFs, handbooks).
3. Create support tickets automatically when an issue needs human action.
4. Conversational chat interface available inside Microsoft Teams.
5. Escalate complex queries to the right HR or IT person.

Integrations
------------
- Microsoft Teams (primary channel for employees)
- ServiceNow (for ticket creation and tracking)
- SharePoint (source of HR policy documents)

Constraints
-----------
- Must keep employee data private and comply with data-protection rules.
- Initial budget is limited; LLM cost must be controlled.
- Target go-live within 8 weeks.
- Answers must be grounded in official company documents, not generic web data.
